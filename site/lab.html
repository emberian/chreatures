Development / 001
Measured

Growth becomes physical habitat.

Three related, supplied colony genomes ran for 180 model seconds with native chemistry and MuJoCo geometry. They built over 1,600 branches, roots and leaf surfaces. A restored checkpoint reproduced the subsequent physical, chemical and developmental state exactly in the same runtime.

Live tissue converted to detritus while the scaffold persisted. An experimentally removed leaf transferred its remaining material into another lineage’s gut, where the common reaction system digested it. End-to-end elemental residuals were below 10−12 in the model’s abstract units; the energy residual was also below 10−12.

This establishes joined mechanisms. The founders and their endowments were engineered. The transfer was supplied by the experiment, and the run contained no neural controller. Autonomous predation, reproduction and ecological selection were not demonstrated.

Ecology / 002
Running

Changing terrain changes transport.

Static additions and removals now change the chemical field’s geometry. A newly built solid conservatively displaces covered material; reopening a passage restores fluid space. A physical wall experiment retained total mass exactly, blocked cross-wall transport, then resumed transport after removal.

The field uses a finite grid. Tiny leaves and cracks can fall below its resolution. Displacement is a numerical material-conservation rule, not a fluid pressure or momentum simulation.

Inheritance / 003
Running

Inheritance specifies capacities and construction.

A small genotype compiles into actual enzyme expression, growth dimensions and neural port gains. Offspring carry source and ancestry hashes. Learned memories, current neural activity and adult physiology remain outside the genome.

Mobile morphology can also vary across residents in the same world: actual trunk and limb dimensions, antennae, mass, friction and bounded motor parameters. The current mobile interface remains six-legged. These are mechanisms for diversification, not evidence that useful specializations have evolved.

Prediction / 005
Measured

Personal history can inform imagined futures.

A recurrent predictor trained on 184,320 aligned transitions from actual full-graph developmental runs. Whole worlds were held out. The trained model beat persistence on feature and physiology prediction at the reported horizons; a Rust implementation reproduces its forecasts closely and maintains private recurrent state.

Changing the supplied action history changed forecast quality. The strongest discrimination was in neural features and angular motion. Energy, gut contents and support showed much weaker action sensitivity.

Private counterfactual queries now execute. Useful planning and improved long-term regulation remain unestablished. A plausible forecast is not sufficient evidence to trust a policy built around it.

Behavior / 006
Still open

More neurons have not solved foraging.

Short developmental probes have shown effects of neural input and learning. Longer resident observations still expose reserve depletion and ineffective activity. Sustained autonomous feeding, useful manipulation and durable individual strategies remain unmet capabilities.

Biological wiring also has to earn its role: a matched rewired graph performed better on ingestion in one earlier controlled comparison. That makes the controls informative and the architecture revisable.

Derived anatomy / 007
Measured

A circuit can acquire a traceable descendant.

A blueprint duplicated a 3,627-neuron learning-circuit template and its declared incoming and outgoing connections. The resulting graph has 168,749 neurons and 26,557,080 directed edges. All original neuron metadata and ancestral-to-ancestral edges remained exact.

The derived graph executed on AMD hardware, and its duplicate cells responded to sensory input. Added outputs also changed incoming normalization on 3,152 ancestral targets, which the artifact records explicitly.

This establishes executable structural variation. The copy is a synthetic intervention; useful specialization, developmental biology and behavioral improvement remain unestablished.

Social life / 008
Still open

From company to interdependence.

Shared physics, sensory signals and persistent structures provide places to begin. Embodied reproduction, dependent young, resource-funded nursery care and evolved divisions of labor are still to be built.

We plan to compare individual and group outcomes, hold resource budgets constant, intervene on communication and construction cues, and distinguish learned task preferences from reproductive specialization.
