Reciprocal-wave recording. The embedded 360-frame episode is an authenticated research continuation from a coherent tick boundary, not a fresh birth. It exposes eight residents' recorded sensory and physical streams plus exact typed event receipts without exposing private memory content.

Two completed reciprocal waves. All 160 assigned research lives completed 2,048 ticks across ten environments: 327,680 resident transitions. The principal wave took 986 supervised seconds; the selected challenge wave took 969.03 seconds. They recorded five and sixteen mouth-contact ticks respectively. The cohorts differ, so this is not a measured improvement in feeding.

Connected evidence. The native Weave graph now contains 9,753 nodes and 28,802 edges, linking the earlier campaign, fitted mechanisms, explicit research branches and 9,081 recorded world events. Original failed histories remain preserved.

Archive and fits. After both waves, the population search holds 74 entries in 21 archive cells, informed by 160 completed life records. Fitted GAM models contribute bounded action scores and private corrections. Their predictive accuracy does not by itself establish a behavioral benefit. The inherited research controller is downloadable.

What remains open. This recording establishes that the joined mechanisms ran and records their physical consequences. A longer private continuation later paused after a material packet escaped the finite terrain; its checkpoint and diagnosis are preserved. It does not by itself demonstrate learned feeding, cross-feeding, sustained regulation, social competence, reproductive success or evolutionary improvement.

All evidence and limitations  ↗
  1. Earlier reference

    Compact FlyWire world

    A 6,789-neuron female FlyWire v783 subset powered the earlier 2D engine and bootstrap behaviors. It remains an explicitly separate archived substrate.

  2. Physical ecology

    Growth became persistent terrain

    Three supplied colony lineages built more than 1,600 physical parts in 180 model seconds. Material transfer, digestion, topology-sensitive transport and deterministic continuation were exercised in controlled runs.

  3. Embodiment

    Brain, cord, body and chemistry joined

    Full MaleCNS dynamics reached articulated residents with physical feeding, energy expenditure, private recurrent state and continuous action.

  4. Population v4

    The first campaign closed with mixed evidence

    Sixteen evaluations completed, while two shared cohort-engine faults affected 64 other records. The archive and fitted response surfaces are inspectable; learned feeding, durable regulation and evolutionary improvement remain unproven.
