One coupled world

The interesting part happens between the organs.

Chreatures is built around consequences that cross boundaries. A movement changes what the eyes can encounter. Activity draws on finite reserves. A colony changes collision geometry and chemical transport. Solar position changes illumination and phototrophic energy. Another body, a moved object, a sound, or a signal can enter the next private sensory state.

01

A measured neural scaffold

MaleCNS v1.0 supplies 165,122 traced brain-and-cord neurons, 25,563,197 directed edges, and 124,025,046 measured synapses represented by those edges. Native recurrent dynamics run against that anatomy; the dynamics and interfaces are engineered.

02

Vision from the body

The physical head carries 1,024 collision rays: an 8 × 32 peripheral field and a 24 × 32 central field, each with RGB and proximity. Gaze and full body rotation move the lens. Bodies, branches, terrain and objects can occlude it.

One private history per life

Each resident's 4,459-value input joins rich vision, 351 physical channels and twelve physiological channels. Experienced four-frame encounters can become remembered goals. Recurrent state, memories, learning and random state remain private to that life.

Twelve ways to act

Thrust, yaw, gaze pitch and posture combine with grip, three signal bands, eating, release, secretion and allocation. Inherited GAM laws and body-dependent neural gains may vary between candidates; delivered action and bodily consequence can update only the individual's bounded private corrections.

05

Development costs material

Energy, gut, fatigue, structural integrity, neural support, gland and brood stores, maturity and exchange load are physical state. Secretion and clonal allocation draw from body-held material. Hatching changes world topology; it does not transfer a parent's private memory.

Worlds have ancestry too

Regional grammars generate platforms, ramps, underpasses, shelter and finite resources. The ten current environments contain 15–35 recorded regions and 22–52 physical links. Their analyst geometry is public evidence and never becomes a controller input.

Built, running, unresolved

The population wave is an experiment, not a result announced early.

Current cohort. The M2 recording shows eight v4 founders with varied bodies in one generated regional habitat, all using the full MaleCNS substrate. It records direct senses, actions and post-physics body measurements; it does not expose private memory content.

Current campaign snapshot. Eighty candidate lives are assigned across ten authenticated environments. The public archive currently contains 81 genome records, ten environment records, and zero completed evaluations. Pending assignments are not scored lives.

Variation with boundaries. Candidates can inherit body parameters, MaleCNS interface gains and completed GAM law fits. Environment genomes vary regional grammar and resources. Private neural state, remembered experience and learning do not cross a birth.

What remains open. The new recording shows a functioning coupled world. It does not demonstrate learned feeding, sustained regulation, social competence, reproductive success or evolutionary improvement. Those findings require terminal lives and comparisons that are still running.

Prior predictive courtyard. The earlier 240-frame courtyard episode remains available as a separate prior-life recording. It documents the v3 proposal, forecast and GAM path and precedes that world's later physical-source failure; it is not evidence from the current v4 campaign.

Earlier lineage evidence. The completed developmental grandchild and the first Living Reef biography remain in the lab chronology. They establish particular executed mechanisms and failures without being silently reinterpreted as current lives.

  1. Earlier reference

    Compact FlyWire world

    A 6,789-neuron female FlyWire v783 subset powered the earlier 2D engine and bootstrap behaviors. It remains an explicitly separate archived substrate.

  2. Physical ecology

    Growth became persistent terrain

    Three supplied colony lineages built more than 1,600 physical parts in 180 model seconds. Material transfer, digestion, topology-sensitive transport and deterministic continuation were exercised in controlled runs.

  3. Embodiment

    Brain, cord, body and chemistry joined

    Full MaleCNS dynamics reached articulated residents with physical feeding, energy expenditure, private recurrent state and continuous action.

  4. Current wave

    Lives and regions began to branch

    The v4 interface joins twelve actions and twelve physiological inputs with inherited body and neural variation, private development, materially funded clonal birth and native quality-diversity evaluation. The first 80-life campaign is running; its terminal findings are not yet known.
