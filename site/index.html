The current architecture couples a varied articulated body, the full 165,122-neuron MaleCNS graph, lifetime and recent-sequence memory, twelve-action forecasting, and material stores that fund exchange, local growth and brood development. Physical fermentate packets let consequences move between bodies and places without giving a controller privileged world labels.

These mechanisms are implemented. A fresh recording of the reciprocal wave is still being authenticated. The public observatory below preserves the earlier trained-organ episode, while the population atlas preserves the completed 80-life campaign and its failures.

One reciprocal world

Actions become matter, memory and another body's next sensation.

Movement changes what the eyes can encounter. Activity draws on finite reserves. Released fermentate persists as physical packets that can move, collide and be acquired. Funded development changes nearby geometry. Those consequences return through ordinary vision, contact, physiology and signals; each resident keeps its own recurrent state and remembered history.

01

A measured neural scaffold

MaleCNS v1.0 supplies 165,122 traced brain-and-cord neurons, 25,563,197 directed edges, and 124,025,046 measured synapses represented by those edges. Native recurrent dynamics run against that anatomy; the dynamics and interfaces are engineered.

02

Vision from the body

The physical head carries 1,024 collision rays: an 8 × 32 peripheral field and a 24 × 32 central field, each with RGB and proximity. Gaze and full body rotation move the lens. Bodies, branches, terrain and objects can occlude it.

03

A private history at two scales

Lifetime context and recent sequences are carried alongside recurrent state. Experienced encounters can become remembered goals; short sequences preserve how a situation unfolded. Memory content, learning state and random state remain private to each life.

Forecast all twelve actions

The H4 predictor covers thrust, yaw, gaze, grip, three signal bands, posture, eating, release, secretion and allocation. Three inherited GAM response laws can refine candidate consequences within their fitted domains; predictions and associations do not by themselves prove useful choice.

Implemented, executed, unresolved

The mechanism is current. The evidence keeps its date.

Reciprocal-wave architecture. Lifetime and recent-sequence memory, H4 twelve-action forecasting, a three-law GAM bank, physical fermentate packets, locally funded growth and environment search v2 are implemented. Its new public life recording is still pending authentication.

Earlier public recording. The embedded episode records eight residents using the preceding predictor and two-law bank. It records direct senses, actions and post-physics measurements; it does not expose private memory content or establish reciprocal-wave behavior.

Completed campaign. Sixteen of 80 evaluations completed 24,000 ticks. Two shared cohort-engine faults affected the other 64 allocated records; these are infrastructure failures, not 64 independent biological deaths. The authenticated evidence graph contains 629 nodes and 1,483 edges.

Archive and fits. Twelve admission decisions were retained, with nine members in the final archive. Three fitted response laws now exist across the evidence, including a complete-B2 association fit. A fitted association is not a causal law or proof that a resident used it well.

What remains open. No public result yet demonstrates learned feeding, cross-feeding, sustained regulation, social competence, reproductive success or evolutionary improvement. Those findings require new lives and controlled comparisons.

All evidence and limitations  ↗
  1. Earlier reference

    Compact FlyWire world

    A 6,789-neuron female FlyWire v783 subset powered the earlier 2D engine and bootstrap behaviors. It remains an explicitly separate archived substrate.

  2. Physical ecology

    Growth became persistent terrain

    Three supplied colony lineages built more than 1,600 physical parts in 180 model seconds. Material transfer, digestion, topology-sensitive transport and deterministic continuation were exercised in controlled runs.

  3. Embodiment

    Brain, cord, body and chemistry joined

    Full MaleCNS dynamics reached articulated residents with physical feeding, energy expenditure, private recurrent state and continuous action.

  4. Population v4

    The first campaign closed with mixed evidence

    Sixteen evaluations completed, while two shared cohort-engine faults affected 64 other records. The archive and fitted response surfaces are inspectable; learned feeding, durable regulation and evolutionary improvement remain unproven.
