The paired blank-screen run

The film changes the loop.

The film and blank-screen runs begin with identical recorded neural rates, latent states, actions and positions. The film subsequently changes 64,121 non-photoreceptor neurons above a 10⁻⁶ difference threshold, followed by motor commands and physical trajectories. This is one paired seed, with an untrained motor controller—not a demonstration of recognition or useful learned behavior.

The centre panel shows rate changes since its first sample, including ongoing internal dynamics. Cyan means an increase; orange means a decrease. The scale stays fixed for the entire film and clips the largest 0.5% of changes at measured soma locations. All 165,122 neurons simulate; 140,024 have measured soma positions available to plot.
