# Live CNS wave: delivered state and next frontier

The September 7 wave delivers a new, actually local browser life: official MuJoCo
Wasm contact physics, Rust/Wasm sensory and private cognitive state, and WebGPU
recurrence across 165,122 MaleCNS neurons and 25,563,197 measured directed edges.
The [research data and corrected controller release](https://github.com/emberian/chreatures/releases/tag/embodied-learning-v2-research-20260907)
preserves the learning experiments separately from the public default.
The public entry is `live.html`; `live.html?stimulus=bad-apple` selects the physical
film screen after the visitor starts a fresh life. Earlier ecological recordings
are separate historical worlds. They never supply the live neural animation.

## What is coupled now

Three articulated bodies share a constructed 3D garden, finite resources,
physical objects, a video surface and propagating sensory signals. Body movement
changes anatomical-order retinal rays and 43 nonvisual afferents. Those enter
trainable CNS afferents, actual measured recurrence and a learned CNS readout.
Input-afferent rows are excluded from the readout. Private recurrent memory,
experienced goals, acquired action suffixes and learned proposal selection see
only that readout, their own delivered actions and reset/timing state.

Visitors can switch the physical screen, send a three-note greeting, add or shove
an object, inspect the bilateral retinal facets and whole-CNS signed activity,
and save/load the whole interacting state. These interactions are implemented
sensory events, not evidence that residents have learned social relationships.
The enlarged hexapod, eye angles, neural dynamics and physiological laws include
explicit engineering choices. Browser ingestion currently uses a root-radius law;
precise native mouth contact and regional ecology remain a later engine epoch.

V2 dynamics have a positive operating point, signed adaptation and bounded local
support. Actual native GAM fits characterized a 27-setting full-graph sweep and
predicted a subsequently executed setting. Native Universal Weave carries the
experiments, competing interpretations and failed candidates. Rust SIMD reduced
complete measured three-body tick time by 32%; selected retina and neural state
now reach the observer every physical tick. M2 headless execution and exact
coupled continuation passed. Browser UI performance has not been measured.

## What the runs established

The 30-second film-versus-blank experiment started from identical physical, full
CNS and private resident/RNG states. For observed resident 0, 1,370 supported
retinal sites and 140,200 non-afferent rows differed at some point above 1e-6.
Commands and physical paths diverged as well. This establishes a physical
stimulus-dependent loop, not film understanding, recovered physiology or welfare.
The [public response figure](../../site/assets/live-cns-screen-response.svg) and
[native evidence graph](../../site/assets/live-cns-evidence.json) preserve scope.

Eight new three-body worlds contributed 12,288 physical teacher transitions,
including failed maneuvers. Six worlds train; two are held out. A 256-update
causal controller fit on persvati improved held-out action error by 51% and
memory loss by 19%. The predictor now uses only a current experienced key;
future achieved keys are reserved for inverse-action hindsight learning. An
already completed fit with a forecasting shortcut remains labeled and preserved.

Actual autonomous outcomes were much less encouraging than those offline
losses. Sampling learned proposals increased effort and reduced food outcomes.
Using the existing MAP mode reduced that candidate's effort about 61% in the
first matched context and improved stopping. In three new contexts, MAP again
stopped more often, but still spent more effort, lost more reserves and acquired
less net resource than the initialized parent. These are comparisons of autonomous
behavior, not externally commanded goal attainment. No trained controller was
promoted into the public default on this evidence.

An additional hbox run used the actual afferent sidecars to examine the CNS.
Procedural visual-prediction success transferred weakly to the chosen physical
probes. A bounded 90-update full-CNS fine tune gave a small body-current gain;
four-tick change predictions remained near persistence. Its current probe was
linear and its GRU64 future probe omitted commands, so this does not establish
absence of nonlinear body information or failure of action-conditioned prediction.
The research candidate remains separate. See
[the physical-CNS probe](../../research/physical_cns_probe/README.md).

## The next integrated development wave

The important frontier is learning to use the coupled organism, with richer
worlds supplying experience. More offline action imitation alone is unlikely to
resolve the mismatch we observed.

1. **Learn from consequences under the resident's own goals.** Continue the
   native recurrent controller and private goal reservoir, and train selection
   and termination on actual rollouts under that manager. Preserve failed
   encounters. Hindsight demonstrations initialize proposals, but cannot alone
   teach when an autonomous resident should choose them.
2. **Improve body information through the CNS.** Calibrate existing afferents
   and readouts against actual sensory ranges, with nonlinear and
   action-conditioned models where their function calls for them. Reuse sealed
   raw sensory streams for explicitly reconstructed neural features. Never
   relabel an old life or route raw body state around the CNS.
3. **Rejoin the ecological mechanisms in the canonical Rust world.** Bring
   regional finite-material transport, funded construction, body variation and
   development into the live world alongside memory and interaction. A later
   browser epoch can also adopt precise mouth acquisition. That maneuver must
   not become a gate that postpones the other pillars.
4. **Give the population more ways to encounter and change its habitat.** Use
   connected three-dimensional routes, movable occluders, material-building
   colonies and socially generated signals. Let inherited dynamics, acquired
   memories and constructed surroundings create distinct viable strategies;
   measure whole lives and contexts instead of selecting on adjacent ticks.

Continue one current production mechanism, content-addressed live assets,
immutable model identities and private complete checkpoints. The public model
remains the trained V2 CNS with initialized resident control. Frozen native lives
and unrelated node jobs were not advanced or replaced by these experiments.
