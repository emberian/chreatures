# Physical worlds and their sensorimotor interfaces

Latest user steering takes precedence over the initial flat-world design: use a genuinely complicated 3D environment, substantial backend progress before verification, rich social and outside interaction, and mechanisms that leave room for discovery. The first FlyWire garden now runs from its pinned archive; current production is 3D. The target dataset is MaleCNS brain plus cord, with full curated connectivity acquired and benchmarked remotely rather than quietly advertised through a tiny female-brain subset.

The direction: a little ecosystem of physical opportunities. Raised ground and ramps, sheltered underpasses, light and shade, rolling and stackable objects, resonant objects, a balance bridge, pendulums, resources that can be carried, and residents whose activity changes what others encounter. Physics creates combinations; predefined gameplay quests do not delimit the space. The human has a physical hand/spring, sound and light instruments, and can offer, move or release objects. Residents receive the hand through sensation. Social behavior can develop through visible motion, contact, signaling and shared artifacts without injecting private account/creature IDs into cognition.

## Implementation seam

`chreatures/physics.py`: `PhysicsWorld(seed=7, spec=None)` with actual MuJoCo 3D dynamics, z-up meters. No browser physics substitution. PhysicsWorld owns native model/data, entity spec/component data, physiology and RNG. Serialize full MuJoCo integration state (`mjSTATE_INTEGRATION`) plus model specification, mutable geom/material/constraint state, component values, schedules and RNG. Never silently load a different engine/source revision as exact continuation.

`data/habitats/hollow-garden.json`: declarative entity/material/component definitions. Entity shape, physical material and attached biological components are independent fields. Supported geometry is a composable set, not an enumeration of objects with hand-coded intentions. Include static boxes/ramps/arches, free spheres/boxes/capsules, hinged seesaw and resonant pendulum, food/scents attached to physical objects. Limits are numeric/capacity constraints; no world script makes residents seek food or play.

The habitat compiler has an explicit `compiler.material_order`. MuJoCo material indices therefore survive canonical JSON writers that sort object keys. Restore also recovers the declaration order of early version-1 checkpoints from their saved `mat_rgba` table before applying the model-signature check. This is a compatibility reconstruction, not a relaxed check: the rebuilt model must still reproduce the saved signature.

The supplied bodies include a synthetic traction-based crawler and an articulated six-legged body with twelve physical hinges. Inherited traits change actual segment geometry and motor parameters. Body mechanics and stabilization are engineered capabilities. The articulated body is not an integrated NeuroMechFly model; see [ARTICULATED.md](ARTICULATED.md) and [BODY_INHERITANCE.md](BODY_INHERITANCE.md).

The current 351-channel interface includes body-oriented occluded retinal rays, scent, sound, contact and proprioception, with 384 anatomical neural readouts. `advance(actions, dt)` applies bounded forces and joint control, advances native physics, and returns actual contact, movement and physiological outcomes. `view()` exposes physical geometry and joint poses for the renderer. Exact persistence covers model identity, native integration state, mutable physical state and all owned cognitive/ecological systems. See [NEURAL_PORTS.md](NEURAL_PORTS.md), [SOMATIC_PHYSIOLOGY.md](SOMATIC_PHYSIOLOGY.md) and [ECOLOGICAL_EXCHANGE.md](ECOLOGICAL_EXCHANGE.md).

Caregiver commands: add physical entity from shape/material/components preset, move by bounded force/spring through a cursor hand (not instant relocation while time advances), release, impulse within physical bounds, signal at3D position, light. Authoring spec changes are separate from lived actions. Object operations and simulation advancement remain ordered by one authority.

## Connected terrain and local light

The high walk is a connected circulation system rather than a floating slab. Its west approach is a 6-degree, 6.3-meter physical ramp. The ramp's lower face is buried below the floor and its upper face overlaps the deck, so there is no vertical toe or crest gap for a small foot or body shell to catch. Low rails continue into the deck guards. The broad deck has a second inclined connector to an upper lookout, while the historical `east-ramp` entity is a south-facing return to the floor. These stable entity IDs remain grouped for developmental scene transforms.

The long approach also creates a traversable cross-route beneath its raised eastern half. A separate hollow arch provides a taller shaded underpass, and an open-sided timber nook adds a low shelter that remains accessible from the east and south. Its entrance leaf is a real limited hinge with damping and resonance, so bodies and shared objects can push it. Food inside the nook, food on the ramp, balls, boxes, the seesaw and the pendulum all remain ordinary physical entities whose shape, mobility, material and components compose independently.

`light` is an attached component with a body-local position and direction plus color, intensity and sensory radius. The compiler emits native MuJoCo lights, `.view().lights` reports their current world poses for renderers, and sensory illumination uses distance, direction and physical ray occlusion. The cyan underpass lamp and amber lookout lamp produce distinct local conditions; the temporary caregiver light contributes through the same scalar illumination channel.

The traversal smoke check used the twelve-hinge `ArticulatedWorld`, joint torques and terrain contact only. Starting on the floor west of the ramp, Mica reached the deck at `(6.70, 2.20, 0.71)` after paced locomotion with yaw correction. Sustained maximum gait drive still accumulates fatigue and can stall on the slope, so controllers must use proprioception and recovery rather than expecting the habitat to carry a resident uphill.

## Coupling and learning work

Current developmental worlds train a population-shared predictive actor–critic from measured circuit activity and embodied state. Fresh residents can inherit the motor policy while retaining private context, action–outcome memory and lifetime plasticity. Short experiments show neural effects on behavior, but sustained acquisition, regulation and useful manipulation remain unfinished. The archived 2D attraction/avoidance rules are not the current production controller. Training and held-out interventions must distinguish real capability from incidental contact and initial physiological endowments.

Native GAM and Weave already execute on actual project state; integrate their results where they help investigation. Avoid growing dashboard taxonomy faster than the organism. The next capabilities should extend reciprocal learning and physical possibilities, with meaningful integration checks.
